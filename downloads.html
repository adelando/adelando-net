📖 How to Install ▾
Step 1 Select your version and download the archive
Step 2 Unzip the archive and pick the worlds you want to play
Step 3 Drag and drop the world folder into .minecraft/saves
Step 4 Open the Minecraft Launcher and create or switch to a profile matching the downloaded version
Step 5 Launch Minecraft → Singleplayer → find your world → Edit → Game Rules
Step 6 Set doFireTick and doTNTDamage to false, then press Play!
